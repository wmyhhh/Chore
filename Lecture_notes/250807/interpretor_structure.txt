Eval
    base cases:
        primitive values (numbers)
        look up value bond to symbols
    recursive calls:
        eval(operator, operands) of call expressions
        apply(procedure, arguments) 
        eval sub-expressions of special forms (if...)
Apply
    base cases:
        built-in primitive procedures
    recursive calls:
        eval (body) of user defined procedures

Special forms

Logical forms
    if expression：
        evaluate the predicate -- do_if_form
        choose a sub-expression -- do_if_form
        evaluate that sub-expression in place of the whole expression -- scheme_eval
    and & or
    cond expression
